Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Ask us.

What is MAMA? expand_more

MAMA is a maternal-care companion. You can log how you feel, track medicines and appointments, keep a support circle of family in the loop, and prepare a summary to share with your doctor.

Does MAMA replace my doctor? expand_more

No. MAMA helps you keep track and notice changes, but it does not diagnose or treat anything. Always follow your doctor's or midwife's advice. If you have severe pain, heavy bleeding, reduced baby movements, a severe headache or trouble breathing, call your doctor or local emergency number (112 in India) straight away.

Who can see my health information? expand_more

You can. People only see what you choose to share: members of your support circle you invite, and a doctor you send a time-limited brief to. You decide what goes into a brief, such as vitals, medicines, symptoms, mood or notes.

How is my data protected? expand_more

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, free-text notes are encrypted before they are saved, and traffic between your device and MAMA is sent over HTTPS. You can read the details in our Privacy Policy. Read the Privacy Policy.

How do medicine reminders work? expand_more

Add a medicine yourself or scan a prescription, check the suggested times, and MAMA sends reminders on those times. You can mark each dose as taken so your doctor brief shows how things have gone.

How do I add my partner or family? expand_more

Open Support and invite someone by email or link. They can follow along and send you encouragement, and you can remove them at any time.

Can I log by voice? expand_more

Yes. On the My Health page you can speak a short note instead of typing it, which is handy when your hands are full or you are tired.

How do I delete my account and data? expand_more

Send us a message from the Contact page and we will delete your account and the information linked to it. See the Privacy Policy for how long we keep data. Contact us.
