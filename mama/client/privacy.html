1. Who we are

MAMA (“we”, “us”) is a maternal-care companion app and website. This policy explains what personal information we handle when you use it. Questions? Use our contact form or write to support@YOUR-DOMAIN.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us:

  • Account details: your name, email address, optional phone number and password (stored only as a one-way hash).
  • Pregnancy details: your due date or stage, and your preferences.
  • Health records you choose to log: mood, symptoms, blood pressure, weight, temperature, baby kicks and free-text notes (notes are encrypted before they are stored).
  • Medicines, doses and appointments you add, including details read from a prescription image you scan.
  • Voice notes you record, which are turned into text.
  • People you invite to your support circle (their name and email or contact details).
  • Messages you send through our contact form.

Information collected automatically:

  • Basic technical data such as browser type, device type and pages visited, through Google Analytics if it is enabled (see section 6).
  • A sign-in token and your profile summary kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the app: show your pregnancy progress, store your logs, send medicine and appointment reminders, and build summaries for you to share.
  • To notice patterns and show gentle alerts when readings you log may be worth discussing with a doctor. These are not diagnoses.
  • To keep MAMA secure and prevent spam and abuse.
  • To reply to your messages and improve the service.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use your health information for advertising.

4. Who can see your information

Only you, unless you choose otherwise:

  • Support circle. People you invite can see what the app allows for circle members and can send you nudges. You can remove them at any time.
  • Doctor brief. If you create a share link, anyone with the link can view the selected sections until it expires or you delete it. Share it only with people you trust.
  • Service providers. We use trusted providers to run MAMA: hosting and database providers, an email provider for invites and replies, push-notification services, and Google's Gemini AI service to turn voice notes into text and to read prescription images and notes. They process data only to provide those services.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

5. How we protect it

  • Connections between your device and MAMA use HTTPS.
  • Passwords are hashed; free-text notes are encrypted at rest.
  • Access to the service is controlled by sign-in, and sign-in and contact attempts are rate-limited.
  • No system is perfectly secure. Please use a strong, unique password and sign out on shared devices.

6. Cookies, local storage and analytics

MAMA does not use advertising cookies. It stores a sign-in token and profile summary in your browser's local storage, which is cleared when you sign out. If Google Analytics is enabled, it collects anonymised usage statistics (IP anonymisation is on and advertising features are off). We do not load analytics if your browser sends a Do Not Track signal. You can also block analytics with a browser extension.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete the personal information linked to it within a reasonable period, except where we must keep something by law. Contact messages are kept for up to 12 months.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correct or export the information we hold about you.
  • Delete your account and data.
  • Withdraw your consent, for example by removing someone from your support circle or turning off reminders.
  • Complain to the relevant data-protection authority if you are unhappy with how we handle your data.

To use any of these, contact us and choose “Delete or export my data”.

9. Children

MAMA is intended for adults. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

10. Medical disclaimer

MAMA is not a medical device and does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always speak to your doctor or midwife about your health. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in India).

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by email.
